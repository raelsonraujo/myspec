## Definition

Change risk of each developer-controlled method, combining cyclomatic complexity and line coverage: `CRAP(m) = CC(m)² × (1 − cov(m))³ + CC(m)`, where `CC` is the method's cyclomatic complexity and `cov` its measured line coverage as a fraction.

## Gates

| Reported value | Threshold | Applies to | Enforcement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Highest CRAP score among methods | `≤ 30` | Both | Attention |
| Methods above 30 | `= 0` | Both | Attention |

This target matches the default boundary of the repository-pinned ReportGenerator, which presents a CRAP score above 30 as elevated. A method above 30 remains an improvement candidate because its combination of complexity and measured coverage presents elevated change risk.

For every method above this gate or the Cyclomatic Complexity gate, report both measured values and the relevant coverage evidence, and classify it using the policy in `my-specs/metrics/cyclomatic-complexity.md`. Distinguish that classification from ReportGenerator's colors, which evaluate each metric independently.

## Decisions and Tradeoffs

- Read the score from ReportGenerator instead of computing it: the report uses the same complexity and coverage data the other metrics record.
- Use `Attention` gates: an elevated score is an improvement signal rather than a blocked inspection until the team explicitly adopts `Blocking` gates.
